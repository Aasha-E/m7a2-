Renewable Energy Products for a Greener Future

At HT Green, we supply a range of renewable energy products designed to help Australian homes and small businesses reduce their electricity costs and become more energy independent. From solar panels and battery storage to wind turbines and small-scale hydro systems, we can help you find the right solution for your property and energy needs.

Solar Panels

Solar panels are one of the easiest ways to start generating your own clean electricity. HT Green supplies high-quality solar photovoltaic (PV) panels that convert sunlight into usable electricity for your home or business.

Our solar panels are designed to provide reliable energy for years to come and can help reduce your dependence on electricity from the grid. Combined with battery storage, a solar PV system can provide even greater energy independence by storing excess electricity for use when the sun isn't shining.

Whether you need a small residential system or a larger solution for your business, HT Green can help you choose the right solar panels for your needs.

Battery Storage

Don't let your excess solar energy go to waste. Battery storage systems allow you to store electricity generated during the day and use it when you need it most.

HT Green offers modern battery storage solutions designed to work with solar PV systems. By storing excess energy, you can reduce the amount of electricity you need to purchase from the grid and make better use of the renewable energy your system produces.

Battery systems are also a useful option for people who want greater energy independence or additional protection against power interruptions.

Wind Turbines

If your property has suitable space and reliable wind, a wind turbine can be an effective way to generate renewable electricity.

HT Green can supply wind turbine solutions for suitable residential, rural and small-business properties. Wind turbines can operate day and night when conditions are right, making them a useful addition to a solar PV system.

Our team can help you understand whether your property is suitable for wind power and what type of turbine would best suit your energy requirements.

Small-Scale Hydro

Have a suitable creek or flowing water source on your property? Small-scale hydro systems can turn the natural movement of water into a reliable source of renewable electricity.

HT Green can provide small-scale hydro solutions for properties with appropriate water resources and site conditions. Hydro power can complement solar and wind systems and, where conditions are suitable, provide a consistent source of energy throughout the year.

We can assess your property and help determine whether a small-scale hydro system is a practical option.

Geothermal Solutions

Geothermal energy uses heat from beneath the earth's surface to provide heating and other energy applications.

HT Green can help you explore geothermal solutions for properties where the technology is suitable. Geothermal systems can provide an efficient and reliable way to heat homes while reducing reliance on conventional energy sources.

Our team can assess your requirements and recommend a solution that works with your property and existing energy systems.

Smart Energy Monitoring

Understanding how and when you use electricity is an important part of reducing energy costs. HT Green offers energy monitoring solutions that help homeowners and businesses keep track of their electricity generation and consumption.

With better information about your energy use, you can identify opportunities to improve efficiency, reduce waste and get more from your renewable energy system.
